VESTA

Vigilance for Extraterrestrial Safety and Thermal Alerts

In space, fire doesn't rise. Smoke drifts, flames hide, and a spark in a high-oxygen habitat can become deadly in seconds. VESTA turns real NASA microgravity combustion experiments into a live monitor for every module: it estimates the chance that a fire ignites and keeps burning, warns crews before it happens, and tells them how to put it out.
